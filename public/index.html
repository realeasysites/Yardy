The OG Story

Downtown Portland's Jamaican OG.

Yardie Ting opened its counter on the second floor of the Public Market House in June 2019 with one mission: authentic Jamaican dishes with a modern twist, made with fresh local ingredients and homemade rubs and spices.

Under owner Shanna-Kay Wright, that little counter in Monument Square became a downtown institution, known for flavorful food, generous portions and friendly service. Yelp even named it among the best spots for Caribbean food in the country.

From di yaad to yuh plate. Respect.
